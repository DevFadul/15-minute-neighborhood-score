About SDG 11 & Methodology

SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities) calls for inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable urban areas with access to green space and affordable, accessible transport. The "15-minute city" concept holds that residents should be able to reach daily essentials within a 15-minute walk, reducing car dependency and emissions. This tool scores a neighborhood from 0–100 across 6 weighted categories.

Manually entered assessments use your own measured or estimated walk times. Assessments created from the landing page's search box use real nearby places (via OpenStreetMap) and a straight-line distance × a 1.3 detour factor at 80 m/min walking pace — a reasonable approximation, not a routed walking distance from a mapping API, so refine them with your own numbers for a more accurate score.

The 3D walkthrough for a searched address is drawn from the same OpenStreetMap data: real building footprints, extruded to their tagged height (or estimated from floor count where no height is recorded), with each facility marker placed at its true distance and compass bearing from the address. Where an area has no buildings mapped, the view falls back to an illustrative street grid so the distances and directions still read clearly.
