About SDG 11 & Methodology

SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities) calls for inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable urban areas with access to green space and affordable, accessible transport. The "15-minute city" concept holds that residents should be able to reach daily essentials within a 15-minute walk, reducing car dependency and emissions. This tool scores a neighborhood from 0–100 across 6 weighted categories.
